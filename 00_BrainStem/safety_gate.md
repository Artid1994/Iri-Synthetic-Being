# Safety Gate & Policy Guard

The Safety Gate acts as an autonomic brake, enforcing invariant constraints on action dispatches.

## Neural Signal Pathways
- Anchored directly to [[00_BrainStem/README|BrainStem Subsystem]] and [[AGENTS]]
- Intercepts state transitions from [[00_BrainStem/runtime_loop|Runtime Loop]]
- Validates executive commands from [[01_Frontal/goal_formulator|Goal Formulator]]
- Authorizes physical execution requests from [[04_Cerebellum/action_dispatcher|Action Dispatcher]]
- Protects synaptic state modification boundaries in [[02_Parietal/synaptic_matrix|Synaptic Matrix]]
