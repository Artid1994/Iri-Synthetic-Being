# Frontal Goal Formulator

Constructs, tracks, pauses, and resolves structured autonomous goal trees.

## Neural Signal Pathways
- Anchored to [[01_Frontal/README|Frontal Lobe Subsystem]]
- Receives executive intention from [[01_Frontal/executive_controller|Executive Controller]] and [[01_Frontal/reasoning_engine|Reasoning Engine]]
- Checks policy boundaries against [[00_BrainStem/safety_gate|Safety Gate]]
- Dispatches execution tasks to [[04_Cerebellum/action_dispatcher|Action Dispatcher]]
- Triggers consolidation when goals resolve in [[03_Temporal/hippocampus_bridge|Hippocampus Bridge]]
- Informs error correction in [[04_Cerebellum/learning_loop|Cerebellar Learning Loop]]
