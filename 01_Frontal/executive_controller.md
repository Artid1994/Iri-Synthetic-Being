# Executive Controller

The Executive Controller arbitrates active attention, prioritizing goals and modulating cognitive cycles.

## Neural Signal Pathways
- Anchored to [[01_Frontal/README|Frontal Lobe Subsystem]]
- Receives clock signals from [[00_BrainStem/runtime_loop|Runtime Loop]]
- Gathers parsed perception from [[05_Occipital/input_pipeline|Perceptual Input Pipeline]]
- Coordinates reasoning bursts with [[01_Frontal/reasoning_engine|Reasoning Engine]]
- Dispatches target plans to [[01_Frontal/goal_formulator|Goal Formulator]]
- Queries episodic retrieval through [[03_Temporal/hippocampus_bridge|Hippocampus Bridge]]
